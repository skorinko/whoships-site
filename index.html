See who ships what, right on your desktop

Whoships shows what needs you, what you're waiting on, who is working on what and what's on fire across your GitHub organization: in widgets, the menu bar and a dashboard that fills your desktop.

Download for Mac €19.99 once · Pricing

Free for 14 days with your own organization, then a one-time purchase. No subscription. Requires macOS 26 or later. Coming soon to the Mac App Store.

What needs you

Reviews requested from you, your PRs with failed CI and the reason why, merge conflicts, and PRs ready to merge.

What's on fire

CI red on main, force pushes and direct pushes to protected branches, unreviewed merges, hotfixes to production.

Who's on what

Each person's branches, open PRs, tickets and what they shipped, plus the most active repos and people.

For team leads and CTOs

Flow health, DORA delivery metrics, security alerts and CI health, from stale PRs to flaky workflows.

Made for the Mac

Widgets in every size, a menu bar summary, a full-desktop dashboard, notifications, light and dark.

Pay once

No subscription. One purchase unlocks Whoships on the Macs you use, with updates to Whoships 1 included. See pricing.

Private

Whoships reads your organization straight from GitHub. Your sign-in stays in your keychain and your GitHub data stays on your Mac; we never see it.
